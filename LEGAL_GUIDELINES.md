# Recording and transcription guidance

AutoScript records and transcribes locally, but recording another person can be
subject to consent laws, workplace rules, and platform terms. Requirements vary
by location and by the participants involved.

## Before recording others

1. Tell every participant that transcription is about to begin.
2. Confirm that each participant can choose not to participate.
3. Use the required Device-capture confirmation only after providing that notice.
4. Follow the rules that apply to every person and platform in the conversation.

The in-app confirmation records an operator statement inside the encrypted
transcript. It is not legal advice and does not establish another person's
consent.

## Protecting transcripts

- Keep `.asenc` files encrypted when sharing or storing them.
- Share the transcript file and its unique token separately.
- Treat a shared token as access to that one transcript.
- An authorized recipient can still read, copy, screenshot, or reshare decrypted
  text. Encryption protects the file in storage and transfer.

## Local operation

AutoScript processes capture and transcription on the local computer. Avoid
sending recordings or transcript text to services you have not reviewed and
approved for the people involved.
