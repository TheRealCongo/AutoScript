# AutoScript Brand and Icon Brief

## Objective

Create a modern, trustworthy visual identity for **AutoScript**, a free, open-source, local-first desktop transcription application. It records selected audio sources and produces local, searchable transcripts. Optional plugins can support Discord voice transcription, speaker labels, and future local translation.

The identity should feel capable and professional in an operations room, meeting, interview, classroom, accessibility, or personal-productivity setting. It must not look military-exclusive, surveillance-oriented, or like a generic cloud AI product.

## Brand idea

The core symbol is a **speech waveform becoming a document**:

- A compact, rounded speech bubble or audio-wave shape represents spoken conversation.
- The waveform resolves into 3–4 clean horizontal document lines, representing speech becoming an organized transcript.
- A subtle checkmark or forward motion may be worked into the negative space only if it remains simple at small sizes.

The mark should work by itself as a Windows application icon and beside the **AutoScript** wordmark. It should communicate "voice to text" immediately without using a microphone illustration, military insignia, headphones, robots, cloud symbols, or literal AI imagery.

## Visual direction

- **Style:** minimal, flat or lightly dimensional vector-style mark; geometric, polished, and calm.
- **Mood:** private, capable, precise, approachable.
- **Primary color:** deep navy / charcoal background with a clear teal-to-blue accent. Suggested accent range: teal `#18B6A4` through blue `#3B82F6`.
- **Supporting colors:** off-white `#F7FAFC`, slate `#1E293B`.
- **Typography:** modern humanist sans-serif; clean, slightly rounded, strong at small sizes. The wordmark must read exactly: `AutoScript`.
- **Background:** create a transparent-background icon version. Also provide dark-background and light-background logo previews.

## Constraints

- No text inside the application icon.
- No tiny details, thin strokes, gradients that disappear at small size, or dense waveform bars.
- The icon must remain recognizable at 16×16 and 32×32 pixels.
- Avoid all third-party logos, brand marks, copyrighted characters, watermarks, and stock-image aesthetics.
- Avoid visual claims of cloud storage, remote monitoring, or data collection. AutoScript is local-first.
- Do not use any previous product name anywhere in the visual.

## Required deliverables

1. **Primary logo:** icon plus `AutoScript` wordmark, transparent PNG, at least 2048×2048 pixels.
2. **App icon master:** icon only, transparent PNG, at least 1024×1024 pixels.
3. **Windows icon:** `.ico` containing 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 128, and 256 pixel sizes.
4. **Optional source:** SVG or another editable vector source.
5. **Preview sheet:** dark-background and light-background versions, including a 32-pixel-size legibility check.

## Copy/paste prompt for GPT or Gemini

```text
Design a polished, vector-style brand identity for a Windows desktop application named "AutoScript". AutoScript is a free, open-source, local-first audio transcription tool: it turns spoken conversation into organized text files on the user's own computer. It can optionally transcribe Discord voice channels and label speakers. The design must communicate "voice becoming text" and privacy-conscious professional software, without looking military-specific or like a cloud AI service.

Create a minimal icon where a rounded speech bubble or compact audio waveform smoothly transforms into a document with clean horizontal transcript lines. Use a deep navy or charcoal foundation with a restrained teal-to-blue accent (#18B6A4 to #3B82F6). The icon should have bold, simple geometry, clean negative space, gentle rounded corners, and remain immediately recognizable at 16x16 and 32x32 pixels. Do not use a literal microphone, headphones, robot, cloud, shield, lock, military insignia, or generic AI sparkle motif.

Create three coordinated outputs: (1) an icon-only mark on a transparent background, with no text; (2) a primary logo with the icon and the exact wordmark "AutoScript" in a modern humanist sans-serif; and (3) a preview sheet on both dark and light backgrounds. No watermark, no third-party logos, no extra slogans, or misspelled text. Favor a crisp, scalable vector logo rather than a photorealistic image.
```

## Acceptance checklist

- The wordmark is exactly `AutoScript`.
- The icon makes sense without the wordmark.
- The icon still reads clearly at Windows tray/icon sizes.
- Transparent asset has a genuine alpha background, not a white checkerboard baked into the image.
- No imagery contradicts the local-first, open-source positioning.
- Deliverables include the icon-only PNG and a multi-size Windows `.ico` file.
